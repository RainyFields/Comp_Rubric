\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A template-diff tokenization bug, specific to the Qwen3 chat template, had removed the header
and leading tokens of every tool observation in our earlier 8B replication of context folding.
Fixing it restores 95--99\% of observations, halves the base model's empty-reasoning rate,
and leaves base accuracy unchanged. Retrained on intact contexts, \foldgrpo{} and \grpo{} both
gain 16--17 points at temperature 1.0 and remain statistically tied at matched budgets, so the
paper's scoring margin again fails to appear at 8B, while the folding mechanism is again
learned. Two behavioral claims are revised: empty reasoning under RL is a reward effect at this
scale, and \foldgrpo{}'s context blow-out advantage does not survive intact observations. The
comparison is bounded by a budget of 46 and 59 reward-bearing steps, recovered from per-step
gradient norms after a shared-queue reclamation ended reward delivery; the checkpoints and
job specifications allow both arms to be resumed to the planned budget. Scripts, per-step logs,
trajectory dumps, judge audit logs, and per-figure data accompany this report.
