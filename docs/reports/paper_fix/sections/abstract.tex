\begin{abstract}
Our earlier replication of context folding \citep{sun2025contextfolding} at 8B scale reported
that \foldgrpo{} and \grpo{} tie on \bcplus{} while the folding mechanism itself emerges. All of
those numbers were produced by an agent whose tool observations were corrupted. The released
re-implementation tokenizes each observation as the difference between two chat-template
renders, and the \qwen{} template removes earlier assistant \think{} blocks whenever a user
turn is appended; the slice is therefore misaligned by the length of the preceding think
block. Every observation lost its role header and its first tokens, 13--16\% were dropped
entirely, and about 80\% of think blocks in the trained policies were empty. The paper's own
model, \seedoss{}, renders history verbatim and is not affected. We fix the bug by rendering
every post-prompt turn standalone and wrapping observations in \toolresp{} tags, which makes
95--99\% of observations intact. On the no-RL base the fix halves the empty-think rate (82\% to
51\%) but leaves accuracy unchanged within its confidence interval. Retraining both arms on
the fixed code, the two arms are again statistically tied: at a matched 40 steps, temperature-1.0
mean@4 is 0.320 (\foldgrpo{}) versus 0.308 (\grpo{}), and at the last uncontaminated
checkpoints 0.335 (46 steps) versus 0.342 (59 steps), each 16--17 points above the base. Two
behavioral claims are revised: with intact observations the empty-think rate still rises under
RL in both arms, so the behavior is a reward effect at 8B rather than a format artifact, and
\foldgrpo{}'s lower context blow-out rate does not survive the fix. The campaign was truncated
at 46 and 59 of 100 planned steps because the reward-serving node was reclaimed by a shared
queue, a limitation we quantify from per-step gradient norms.
\end{abstract}
